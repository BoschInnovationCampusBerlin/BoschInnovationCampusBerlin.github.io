# Key components

* Guidelines for the conversation​
* Customer personas​

# Challenge
Create Voice AI that takes up a specific persona ('Virtual Customer') and problem statement and interacts in a defined scenario with one human. At the end of the interaction, the bot ('Virtual Coach') shall evaluate the conversation based on pre-defined criteria and produce suggestions for improvements.​
